**Disaster Recovery vs Business Continuity Plan**

The business continuity policy outlines the procedures and strategies that an organization should follow to ensure the continuous operation of critical business functions during disruptions or disasters

Disaster recovery is the SPECIFIC steps and protocols for IT infrastructure recovery

**RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**
sets the goal for the time taken to recover business operations after an outage, essential for continuity planning.

**RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**
assesses the maximum tolerable data age for recovery purposes, unrelated to the duration for restoring operations.
