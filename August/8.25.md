# Explain Risk Management Processes

**Risk Identification

*Types of Risk*

Malware Attacks

Phishing Attempts

Insider Threats

Equipment Failures

Software Vulnerabilities 

Nontechnical Risks


**Risk Analysis:

Quantitative - Numeric values to each risk

Qualitive - Subjective Matter. Categories like High, Medium, Low


**Quantitative RIsk Calculations

AV - Asset Value

EF - Exposure Factor
	Percentage of asset's value lost in single incident

SLE - Single Loss Expectancy 
	(AV x EF = cost of ONE occurrence)

ARO - Annualized Rate of Occurrence
	

ALE - Annualized Loss Expectancy
	SLE x ARO - expected cost per YEAR

**Inherent Risk & Heat Maps

Inherent Risk - level of risk before any mitigation has been attempted.

**4 Risk Response

Avoid

Accept

Mitigate

Transfer - insurance, contract with vendor

*Residual Risk*
Left over risk after mitigation


*Risk Appetite*
Acceptable levels of risks

**Risk Management Process: THE RISK REGISTER

Risk Description

Severity

Identified Mitigations

*Risk Threshold*
	Defines limit or levels of acceptable risks. Specific line

*Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)*
	Predictive indicators for monitoring & predicting potential risks

*Risk Reporting*
	Communicates organizations risk profile.


**Business Impact Analysis (BIA)

ID of critical systems & mission essential functions. BIA answers: If disaster strikes, how much time and data can we actually afford to lose
