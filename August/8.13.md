**Wireless Encryption


*Standards

Open - No encryption

DPP (Device Provisioning Protocol) - Replaces WPS

**Intrusion Detection & Prevention Systems


Host-based (HIDS/HIPS) - runs on individual system, access to encrypted traffic. ==OSSEC==

Network-based (NIDS/NIPS) - watches traffic across network segment, no access to encrypted traffic or host itself. ==Snort & Suricata==


==How Do these sensors decide?==

Signature Based - Matches traffic against known patterns of known attacks. ==Weakness = new attacks==

Anomaly Based - Builds statical model of normal and flags deviations. ==Weakness = produces false positives==

Behavioral Based - Flags activity not fitting expected behavior patterns. ==Weakness = generates noise==
