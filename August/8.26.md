
# Data Protection & Compliance Concepts

**Data Types

Regulated Data - Governed

Trade Secrets - Confidential, providing competitive advantage

Intellectual Property - Patents, copyrights

Legal & Financial Data - Contracts, litigation records

**Data Classifications**
	Identifying the importance & associated protections required to protect different types of data


**Data Sovereignty**
	Legal Jurisdiction restricting processing and storage of data on systems that don't physically reside within that jurisdiction
	(==Residency is a choice, Sovereignty is a legal Requirement==)


**Privacy Data**

*GDPR Roles*

Data Controller - decides how data is processed
Data Processor - processes data
Data Subject - the individual the data is about

**Privacy Breaches & Data Breaches**
	Occurs when information is read, modified or deleted without authorization

Data breach is broad, any unauthorized access to data
Privacy breach is the same but for PERSONAL data

**Compliance

*Failures listed below*

Legal Noncompliance - failing to meet law or regulation requirements

Software Licensing - using software beyond terms of its license

Contractual Noncompliance - failing to meet obligation agreed in a contract


**Monitoring and Reporting**
	*Ongoing systemically assessment, evaluation and reporting of adherence to laws, regulations, contracts etc.*

Occurs Internally & Externally
