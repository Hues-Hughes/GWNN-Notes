Account Management
	Provision & De-Provision (typically an HR process)
		Provision
			Request, Verify, Create Account, Give access, Review
			Uses method of least privilege 
			
		De-Provision
			
			Typically automated. Delete user accounts, Revoke active sessions, audit of document/files to send to Manager of person
			Risks related: Orphaned accounts, unauthorized access from old users, database breaches, privilege abuse
